A spacious, upgraded townhouse-style condo in popular McNair Farms Manors — third/fourth floor unit with two full floors of living space. Hardwood-like luxury vinyl on the main level, granite counters in the kitchen and baths, an Elfa-outfitted master closet plus a second walk-in, and vaulted ceilings upstairs. Washer/dryer in-unit, plenty of parking near the entrance, and community pool access in the summer. Less than a two-minute drive to the Dulles Access Road, Route 28, or the Herndon-Monroe Park & Ride — and close enough to Ned Devine's for a cocktail and some dancing on a Friday night. Small pets under 40 lbs welcome with a $750 non-refundable pet deposit.
